What We Do

Four Pillars of
Capital & Operational Expertise

Thompson Capital Group operates at the intersection of consulting, technology, government contracting, and real infrastructure — deploying capital and expertise where others lack the relationships or operational depth to compete.

Our Areas of Focus

Expertise Earned in the Field

Every area TCG operates in represents years of earned expertise — not academic study. Our principals have executed in these domains, built companies within them, and developed the networks that create access others cannot replicate.

01

Strategic Consulting & Advisory

Thompson Consulting

We provide high-conviction strategic advisory to companies navigating growth, operational restructuring, and capital formation. Our principals have advised organizations across government, defense, and private sectors on complex multi-stakeholder decisions — offering the kind of frank, experienced counsel that only comes from operators who have run organizations themselves.

Our advisory work covers organizational strategy, capital structure, executive decision-making frameworks, government relations, and operational transformation. We are not generalist consultants — we bring specific, domain-tested expertise to every engagement.

  • Organizational strategy and restructuring
  • Capital formation and investor relations advisory
  • Government and defense sector navigation
  • Operational transformation and turnaround
  • Executive decision-making and governance frameworks
02

Government & DoD Contracting

Thompson Solutions Group · Thompson Aerospace & Defense

With a proven track record executing multi-million dollar federal and Department of Defense contracts, our team brings the relationships, compliance expertise, and procurement knowledge that institutional partners cannot replicate. We understand the full lifecycle — from market research and solicitation strategy through proposal development, award, and delivery.

Federal contracting is a long game built on credibility, past performance, and relationships that take years to cultivate. The Thompson family's involvement in government contracting spans decades and multiple agencies, providing access and credibility that no newcomer to the space can substitute.

  • Department of Defense (DoD) program support and delivery
  • Federal procurement lifecycle management
  • Proposal development and contract strategy
  • Teaming and subcontracting arrangements
  • Compliance, reporting, and contract administration
03

Enterprise Technology & IT Systems

Thompson Technology Group

TCG deploys capital and expertise into enterprise technology environments — from IT infrastructure and systems integration to digital transformation and managed services. Our principals bring hands-on experience architecting and deploying technology systems at scale, including within federal and defense environments where security and compliance demands are highest.

We look for technology companies at the intersection of government and commercial markets, where defensible IP, long-term contracts, and deep customer relationships create durable value — not the speculative growth multiples that dominate consumer technology investing.

  • Enterprise IT infrastructure and systems integration
  • Federal and DoD IT modernization programs
  • Managed services and cybersecurity capabilities
  • Digital transformation advisory and delivery
  • Technology co-investment and platform building
04

Construction & Infrastructure Investment

Truett Construction · Truett Roofing & Exteriors

Thompson Capital Group is an active operator in commercial construction and roofing through its portfolio companies Truett Construction and Truett Roofing & Exteriors. These are not passive investments — they are operating businesses where TCG principals provide strategic direction, capital for growth, and operational oversight.

The construction and infrastructure sector rewards operators with long-standing vendor relationships, skilled labor access, bonding capacity, and equipment depth — competitive advantages that take years to build and cannot be easily replicated by outside capital. TCG's involvement in this space reflects our operator-first philosophy: we invest in what we understand.

  • Commercial construction project execution and management
  • Roofing, exteriors, and building envelope systems
  • Construction company acquisition and growth capital
  • Infrastructure project teaming and joint ventures
  • Real estate development and construction advisory
