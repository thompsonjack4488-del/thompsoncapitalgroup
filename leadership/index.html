The Family Officer Council

Meet the
Thompson Family

Thompson Capital Group is governed by a close-knit family officer council — Jack, David, and Luke Thompson — carrying forward decades of combined experience across the verticals in which we invest. Every principal has skin in the game. Every voice carries weight.

Active Principals

The Family Officer Group

Each Thompson principal brings a distinct set of capabilities to the firm. Together, they form a governance council that combines enterprise technology expertise, DoD contracting experience, operational leadership, and strategic vision into a cohesive, aligned decision-making body.

Jack Thompson

Jack Thompson
Chief Executive & Primary Capital Allocator

Jack serves as TCG's chief executive and primary capital allocator. With a background spanning enterprise IT systems, strategic advisory, and operational leadership across the firm's portfolio companies, he brings a multi-disciplinary approach to investment decision-making.

Jack is responsible for deal origination, partner relations, and the long-term strategic direction of Thompson Capital Group. He oversees capital deployment decisions across all four of the firm's investment verticals and leads relationships with co-investment partners, institutional investors, and strategic operators.

His hands-on involvement in the firm's operating companies — including Truett Construction and Truett Roofing — reflects TCG's operator-first philosophy: the principals who allocate capital are the same ones who understand how the businesses actually work.

Managing Principal
David Thompson
Managing Principal

David serves as a managing principal of Thompson Capital Group, contributing leadership across the firm's investment and operational verticals. His involvement reflects the family's commitment to multi-generational governance and disciplined capital stewardship.

As a member of the family officer council, David participates in major capital decisions, portfolio company oversight, and strategic planning. His perspective complements Jack's capital allocation focus with a grounding in operational accountability — ensuring that investment decisions are always tested against real-world business realities.

David's role within TCG reflects the family's belief that durable governance requires multiple aligned principals, each with genuine ownership interest and accountability, rather than relying on a single decision-maker.

Luke Thompson
Managing Principal

Luke serves as a managing principal of Thompson Capital Group, bringing a forward-looking perspective to the firm's growth initiatives and portfolio oversight. His leadership within TCG's family officer structure ensures continuity of vision and rigorous oversight across every capital relationship the firm enters.

As the council's member with the broadest focus on growth strategy, Luke is particularly engaged in evaluating new investment opportunities, potential portfolio company acquisitions, and strategic partnerships that can expand TCG's market presence across its four core verticals.

Luke's presence on the council ensures that TCG's decision-making incorporates a long-horizon perspective — keeping the firm anchored to its multi-generational mandate even as it evaluates near-term opportunities and manages current portfolio performance.

In Memoriam
Paul "Rod" Thompson
Founder, Thompson Consulting Group (2004) · The Standard We Carry
"Build something worth building. Honor every commitment. Let your name say the rest."

In 2004, Rod founded Thompson Consulting Group and built it into a respected government contracting and advisory firm through decades of principled leadership. His standard — that the family name on the door is both the firmest commitment and the most fragile asset — remains the bedrock of how Thompson Capital Group operates today.

Rod's legacy is not captured in a revenue figure or a contract value. It lives in the culture he built: one where relationships are honored, where commitments define character, and where the next generation is challenged to build something worthy of the name they inherited. Jack, David, and Luke carry that standard forward every day.
