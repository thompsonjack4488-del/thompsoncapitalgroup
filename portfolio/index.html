Flagship Portfolio Company
Truett Construction

Truett Construction is a commercial construction company operating across the Mid-Atlantic and Southeast United States. Founded under the TCG umbrella, Truett delivers full-scope commercial construction services to institutional, government, and private-sector clients who demand execution quality and schedule reliability above all else.

TCG principals are directly involved in Truett's strategic direction, business development, and capital deployment decisions. The company is built on earned relationships — with owners, general contractors, subcontractors, and suppliers — that provide access to project opportunities and favorable terms that new entrants cannot compete with.

Portfolio Company
Truett Roofing & Exteriors

Truett Roofing & Exteriors provides commercial and residential roofing, siding, and building envelope services across Virginia and the surrounding region. The company has built a reputation for technical quality, responsive project management, and reliability that has earned repeat business from property owners, managers, and developers who need work done right the first time.

TCG views the specialty contracting segment — roofing, exteriors, mechanical — as an attractive category within the broader construction market. These businesses have recurring revenue characteristics (maintenance and inspection programs), high barriers to quality execution, and deep customer loyalty when service quality is exceptional. Truett Roofing embodies this thesis.
