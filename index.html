About the Family

A Legacy Built on Integrity, Execution, and Generational Vision

Thompson Capital Group carries forward a legacy rooted in the founding vision of Paul "Rod" Thompson, who built Thompson Consulting Group in 2004 into a respected government contracting and advisory firm. That culture of disciplined execution and principled leadership now lives in TCG, led by Jack Thompson alongside managing principals David Thompson and Luke Thompson — a tightly governed family officer group with deep expertise spanning enterprise IT, Department of Defense contracting, and large-scale commercial construction.

"We don't chase deals. We build companies, honor commitments, and create value that endures across generations."

This is not a firm assembled from resumes. It is a family with a proven track record across industries where execution matters — where contracts are earned, not awarded, and where operators answer for results with their own name.

Investment Approach

Conservative Capital. High-Conviction Deployment.

We do not chase yield. We identify durable businesses with proven operators, structural competitive advantages, and alignment with long-duration capital. Our investment philosophy is built on the principles that have governed the Thompson family across generations.

01
Operational Accountability First

Every investment we make has an accountable operator behind it. We do not fund ideas — we fund execution. Our principals have operated the businesses we invest in, which means we know the difference between a good pitch and a good company.

02
Long-Term Value Creation

We are generational stewards of capital. Short-term arbitrage is not our mandate. We build lasting enterprise value through operational improvement, strategic positioning, and patient capital deployment across multi-year horizons.

03
Partnership With Proven Operators

We co-invest alongside operators who have skin in the game. Family ownership, executive accountability, and shared downside are non-negotiable components of every capital relationship we enter.

04
Sector Depth Over Breadth

We deploy capital in verticals where we have proprietary knowledge — construction, government contracting, enterprise technology, and infrastructure. We do not venture outside our circle of competence.
