Who Should Reach Out

We Welcome Qualified Inquiries

Thompson Capital Group does not work with everyone who reaches out. We are deliberate about the capital relationships and strategic partnerships we enter — which means when we do accept a partner, they receive the full attention and commitment of the firm. If you fit the profile below, we encourage you to reach out.

Qualified Candidates Include:
  • Accredited investors and family offices seeking alternative allocations in construction or government services
  • Institutional partners evaluating construction and infrastructure exposure with an experienced operator team
  • Strategic operators seeking growth capital and advisory partnership in our core sectors
  • Government and DoD prime contractors seeking teaming arrangements or qualified subcontractors
  • Business owners exploring succession or acquisition by a long-term family owner
  • Private equity firms seeking co-investment in operating companies
What to Expect:
1

Submit your inquiry — use the form to share your background, organization, and the nature of your interest. Include enough context for a principal to evaluate fit.

2

Principal review within 48 hours — all submissions are reviewed personally by one or more firm principals. We do not use staffed intake teams or outsourced screening.

3

Introductory conversation — if there is potential alignment, a Thompson principal will reach out to schedule a confidential introductory call to explore further.

4

Defined next steps — if both parties see a path forward, we define clear next steps, relevant documentation, and a timeline that respects everyone's time.
